The LordOS code is free software under the MIT license, and you can do almost anything with it. The **LordOS name**, the **crown logo** and **Lumo**, the mascot, are different: they tell people that something comes from the LordOS project. This policy explains how you can use them so that nobody is misled.

## What the brand includes

- the name **LordOS** and the wordmark (**Lord**OS, set in Plus Jakarta Sans);
- the crown logo, the app icon, the crown symbol and their variants;
- **Lumo**, the guardian of LordOS, in every pose and drawing.

The official files and the rules for using them (clear space, colours, minimum sizes) are in the [brand kit](https://github.com/lordbasex/LordOS/blob/main/docs/LORDOS-BRAND.md).

## What you can do without asking

- **Refer to the project** by name, for example "runs on LordOS", "a theme for LordOS" or "compatible with LordOS".
- **Link** to lordos.org or the repository, with the name or the unmodified logo.
- **Write about LordOS**: articles, reviews, videos, tutorials, books, talks and classes, using screenshots, the name and the logo.
- **Use the logos and Lumo from the brand kit, unmodified**, following its rules: no stretching, recolouring, other fonts, effects or redrawing.
- **Share unmodified LordOS images** (the ISO, the Raspberry Pi image or the `.deb`) as we publish them, for example on a mirror or a USB stick for a friend.
- **Make community material**, such as stickers or wallpapers for yourself or a user group, as long as you don't sell it.

## What needs our permission

- **Implying endorsement:** don't suggest that the LordOS project or iPERFEX makes, sponsors, certifies or supports your product, service, company or event.
- **Modified distributions:** if you change LordOS (other packages, code, settings or branding) and distribute the result, you can't call it LordOS or use the crown logo or Lumo without written permission.
- **Commercial use of the brand:** selling merchandise with the logo or Lumo, or using them in advertising for a product.
- **Names and domains:** using "LordOS" or something confusingly similar in the name of a product, company, app, distribution or domain name.
- **Modified logos or a modified Lumo**, or new logos that imitate them.

## Forks

Forks are welcome; that's what free software is for. A fork must have **its own name and its own logo**, different from LordOS's and not confusingly similar, and must remove the crown logo, Lumo and the LordOS wordmark from the system and its website. You may say, truthfully, that it is "based on LordOS".

## Asking for permission

Write to us through [our contact form](https://www.iperfex.com/contacto/?lang=en) with what you want to do. We are usually glad to say yes to user groups, events and projects that help people use LordOS.

### Web apps

The [LordOS Web Runtime](/features/web-apps/) and the Store's Web apps catalog name third-party services (WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord and others) so that you can find them. WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord and the other product and service names, logos and icons shown are trademarks of their respective owners. They are used only to identify the services; LordOS is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by them. The catalog's icons are LordOS's own letter tiles; with the web engine installed, LordOS may fetch each site's own icon from the site and show it on your computer, as a browser does. "Tested on LordOS" means only that the LordOS project checked that the site works in the LordOS Web Runtime on that date, not that the service's owner approved anything. LordOS doesn't change the sites: it runs them as they serve them.

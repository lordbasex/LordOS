# Desktops

Keep different work on different desktops: each one has its own windows, and all your displays switch together.
You can have up to nine.

## The Overview

The Overview shows the windows of this desktop and, along the top, all your desktops.

- **Lord Flow:** ⌃↑, or Overview in the Window menu.
- **Lord Classic:** ❖Tab, or the button next to search.

Click a window to go to it. Click a desktop to see it. Press **+** to add a desktop, and the × on a desktop
to close it: its windows move to the next one. Drag a window onto a desktop to move it there.

## Switching

| | Lord Flow | Lord Classic |
|---|---|---|
| Previous or next desktop | ⌃← ⌃→ · ❖⌃← ❖⌃→ | ❖⌃← ❖⌃→ |
| New desktop | In the Overview | ❖⌃D |
| Close this desktop | In the Overview | ❖⌃F4 |
| App windows | ⌃↓ | |

On a touchpad, swipe sideways with three fingers. Four fingers up opens the Overview when four-finger
swipes are on in [Touchpad and touchscreen](help:trackpad).

Choosing a window takes you to its desktop: the Dock, the app switcher and notifications all jump there.

## Moving a window

Window menu → **Move to Desktop**. Apps with their own title bar have it in the menu of a right click on the bar.

## Ctrl and the arrows

In many apps ⌃← and ⌃→ move by word. If you want them for that, turn them off in
[Settings → Keyboard](settings:keyboard); ❖⌃← and ❖⌃→ keep switching desktops.

## Good to know

- A window on another desktop never receives what you type.
- Apps keep running on the other desktops, and a picture-in-picture video stays with you on all of them.
- Your windows come back on their desktops at the next login.

# Calculator

A calculator with buttons and keyboard: addition, subtraction, multiplication, division and
parentheses, with the usual precedence.

- Type with the number pad or the regular keys; **Enter** or **=** gives the result.
- **⌫** deletes the last character and **C** (or Esc) clears everything.
- **%** after **+** or **−** takes that percent of what comes before (200 + 10% = 220, 200 − 10% = 180);
  anywhere else it divides by 100 (200 × 10% = 20). Type **%** on the keyboard.
- **±** changes the sign of the number you are typing (also **F9**).
- It uses your region's decimal separator.

For a quick calculation you don't need to open it: type it in [Search](help:spotlight)
(**❖Space**), which also converts units.

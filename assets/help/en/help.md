# Help

This app. It has an article for every Axia DE app and feature.

- **Search:** the field at the top searches titles and text. Articles also show up in
  [Search](help:spotlight) (**❖Space**).
- **Links:** some open the right Settings section, an app or another article directly.
- **Open the app:** in an app's article, the button at the top opens it.
- **Welcome to Axia DE:** the first-login tour. Watch it again from the sidebar or from the
  **Help** menu in the menu bar.

Articles are in Spanish, English and Portuguese, following the interface language.

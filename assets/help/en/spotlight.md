# Search

**❖Space** (or Ctrl+Space, or the ⌕ magnifier in the bar) opens system-wide search.

## What it finds

- **Apps** (Axia DE, Store and Linux).
- **Files and folders** in your home folder.
- **Settings sections:** “network”, “keyboard”, “wallpaper”…
- **Notes** from the Notes app, by title or text.
- **Articles from this Help.**
- **Clipboard history.**
- **Store apps** you haven't installed yet.
- **Actions:** “dark mode”, “log out”.

## Math and units

Type a calculation and the result appears: `2*(3+4)`, `15% of 2400`, `sqrt(2)`. It also converts:
`10 km to mi`, `72 f to c`, `1.5 gb to mb`. Enter copies the result.

## Keys

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| ↑ ↓ | Choose |
| Enter | Open |
| ❖Enter | Show in Files |
| Tab | Next section |
| Esc | Close |

What you open often moves up in the results.

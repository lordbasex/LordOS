# Store

To install, update and remove apps: Axia DE apps (`.lpk`) and Debian ones.

- **Discover:** Debian desktop apps by category, with their icon and description in your language.
- **Search:** apps come first, then the other packages.
- **Before installing or removing** you see the plan: what gets installed, upgraded or removed,
  how much is downloaded and how much space it takes.
- **Updates:** lists what has a new version and updates everything together. Also in
  [Settings → Updates](settings:updates).
- **`.deb` and `.lpk` files:** open them from [Files](help:files) and they install from here.

Installing or removing system software requires an administrator. Debian signatures are verified
before any list or package is used. `.lpk` apps run isolated and ask for permission to use the
camera, microphone or your files (see [Privacy](help:privacy)).

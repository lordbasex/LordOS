# Install Axia DE

In the live system (the Axia DE USB stick or CD), the **Install Axia DE** assistant copies the system
to the disk.

1. Language, region and time zone.
2. Keyboard, with a field to try it.
3. **Disk:** it is completely erased. To continue you have to tick “I understand the whole disk is
   erased” and type its name.
4. Your user, the password (twice) and the computer name.
5. Summary and **Install**: it partitions, copies, configures and installs the boot loader (UEFI
   and BIOS).

When it's done, “Restart” and remove the USB stick. The installed system starts at the login screen
with your user. This app only exists in the live system.

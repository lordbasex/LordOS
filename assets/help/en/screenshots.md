# Screenshots

Screenshots and screen recording.

| Shortcut | What it does |
|---|---|
| ❖⇧3 · Print Screen | The whole screen |
| ❖⇧4 | An area (with Space, a window; Esc cancels) |
| ❖⇧5 | The screenshot toolbar (while recording: stop) |

- **The toolbar** (❖⇧5) is a small bar at the bottom of the screen: choose the entire screen, a
  window or a selected portion, or record the entire screen or a portion, and press **Capture** or
  **Record**. Its small icons pick a **color** or copy the **text** on the screen. Drag it by its
  grip to move it; Esc closes it, Enter captures, ← → go through its buttons.
- **Options** in the toolbar: where it is saved (Desktop, Documents, Pictures, Clipboard, Mail, the
  markup editor or another folder), a 5 or 10 second timer, the microphone for recordings, the
  floating thumbnail, remembering the last selection (it comes back with handles to adjust it;
  Enter takes it), the mouse pointer in recordings, and your recent captures.
- By default the screenshot goes to `~/Pictures/Screenshots`, and a **thumbnail** shows in the
  corner for a few seconds: click it to open the **editor** and add arrows, boxes, text or blur.
- **Record the screen:** to MP4, with the microphone if you want. ❖⇧5 stops it.
- The first time, the system asks for **screen permission**.

Protected windows (for example password windows) come out black in screenshots, recordings and
remote desktop.
